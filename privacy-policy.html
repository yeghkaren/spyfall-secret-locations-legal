1. Summary

  • The App is a party game played on one device. No account is required, and we do not run game servers.
  • Some settings and the status of your purchases are stored on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. RevenueCat helps us confirm and restore them.
  • The App shows advertising from Google AdMob: banner ads during the game and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to unlock a pack for one round. Banner ads are not shown after you buy "Unlock All Packs".
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices.
  • Location pictures are downloaded from our content delivery network (Cloudflare R2).
  • The App does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • The App is intended for users aged 13 and older.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Information you send us

If you contact us at yeghkaren@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

When you use the Contact Us feature in the App, it prepares an email that may include your anonymous RevenueCat App User ID, App version, and device platform to help us identify purchase-related or technical issues. You can review and edit this information before sending the email.

The App does not ask for your name, password, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

2.2 Information stored on your device

The App stores certain preferences, gameplay settings, cached purchase status, and downloaded images locally on your device:

  • Language preference, to show the App in your chosen language
  • Game setup (number of players and spies, timer, options), to remember your last game
  • Purchase status (whether you unlocked all packs, and which packs you bought), so purchased packs stay unlocked, including when you are offline
  • Downloaded location pictures, so they do not need to be downloaded again

The services built into the App also keep their own data on your device:

  • Google's consent tool saves your privacy choices (in the industry-standard IAB TCF format) so the consent message is not shown again each time, and so ads respect your choices
  • RevenueCat keeps your anonymous App User ID and a cached copy of your purchase status
  • Google AdMob keeps technical data it needs to show ads and limit how often they appear

We do not operate game servers and do not routinely receive your locally stored settings or gameplay information. However, the cached purchase status is a local copy of purchase information that is also processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, as described in section 2.3. When the App can connect, it updates this copy from RevenueCat, so refunds and restored purchases are reflected.

Packs unlocked by watching an ad are not saved. They lock again when the round ends or the App is closed.

This information remains on your device until you clear the App's data or delete the App. Deleting the App does not delete purchase records kept by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat (see section 2.3).

2.3 Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements and to support restoring eligible purchases (for example with Restore Purchases in Settings).

The App does not use a custom user identifier, so RevenueCat generates an anonymous App User ID for your installation. We do not require your name or email address to manage purchases. If you contact us for support and provide your App User ID, we may associate it with your support communication to help resolve your request. Restoring purchases on a new or reset device depends on the store account (Apple ID or Google account) used for the original purchase and on RevenueCat's configured restore behavior; the anonymous App User ID alone does not guarantee restoration across devices.

RevenueCat may process:

  • the anonymous app user ID
  • product identifiers, transaction identifiers, and purchase dates
  • which entitlements are active (for example, a purchased pack)
  • store country, currency, and price
  • technical information needed to provide its service, such as device type, operating system, App version, and IP address

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their own policies and legal obligations.

RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads on the game screen during play. Banner ads load automatically; you do not need to tap anything for them to appear. The App also offers rewarded ads, which are shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for temporary access to a pack.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device identifiers, advertising identifiers (where permitted), diagnostic information, ad impressions, and your interactions with ads, in order to show ads, limit how often they appear, measure their performance, and prevent fraud.

Consent: Where required by law, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to explain how ad-related data is used and to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices. You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices (shown in regions where this is required).

Device settings: On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iPhone and iPad, the App does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so iOS does not make Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) available to the App or to AdMob. AdMob may still process the other information described above, such as IP address and device information, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Because the App is rated for ages 13 and older, the App configures every ad request to allow only ads rated "Teen" (T) or lower under Google's ad content ratings.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google advertising and privacy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

2.5 Location pictures (Cloudflare)

To show location pictures, the App downloads image files from our content delivery network (Cloudflare R2). As with any internet request, Cloudflare may log your IP address, device and browser information (user agent), and the time of the request. We do not use this service to store purchases or any profile about you.

2.6 No analytics or crash reporting

The App does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools (for example Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Sentry). If we add such tools, we will update this Privacy Policy before they are used.

4. Legal Bases (Eea, Uk, And Switzerland)

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the App, the packs you unlock, and your purchases, including confirming and restoring them through RevenueCat, and downloading location pictures.
  • Consent: for advertising that requires your permission, including storing or reading information on your device for ads and showing personalized ads. You give or refuse consent through Google's consent message and can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the App and its content delivery secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and answer support requests. You can object to this processing (see section 8).
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law.

5. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with:

  • Apple and Google, to process payments and distribute the App
  • RevenueCat, to confirm and restore purchases
  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners, to show and measure ads
  • Cloudflare, to deliver location pictures
  • Authorities or other parties, if required by law or to protect our rights

We do not sell personal information for money. However, when ads are personalized, AdMob may use advertising identifiers and similar data for targeted advertising. Some US state privacy laws treat this as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Where these laws apply, you can opt out in Settings > Privacy Choices (when shown) or by limiting ad tracking in your device settings, and the App responds to such choices as required.

6. How Long We Keep Information

  • Information on your device (settings, game setup, purchase status, pictures): until you clear the App's data or delete the App.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide purchase restoration and to meet their legal, tax, and accounting obligations, under their own retention policies.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google and its partners under their own retention policies and settings.
  • Content delivery logs: kept by Cloudflare under its retention settings, typically for a short period for security and operations.
  • Support emails: for as long as needed to answer your request and handle any follow-up, and then deleted, generally within 24 months unless the law requires longer.

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required by law, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • correct inaccurate information

9. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. The App does not require registration and does not ask for names or dates of birth. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Advertising and purchase services in the App may process information as described in this Privacy Policy, and we configure advertising content restrictions to suit the App’s audience (see section 2.4). Additional protections may apply to children and teenagers under applicable laws, including limits on personalized advertising and parental consent requirements.

In some countries, users under 16 (or another age set by local law) need a parent’s or guardian’s permission before consenting to personalized ads. If you are under that age, please ask a parent or guardian before answering the consent message.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at yeghkaren@gmail.com.
